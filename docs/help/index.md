# Slide Viewer Help

Slide Viewer opens whole-slide images of histology slides (NDPI, SVS, TIFF, CZI and more) so you can study them on your own computer. Choose a folder of slides, browse them as thumbnails, and look at each one from the whole section down to single cells. You can adjust how the stain looks, measure structures, and place named pins and shapes.

Pins and shapes are saved as `.ndpa` files next to each slide. That's the format Hamamatsu NDP.view2 uses, so your work opens in NDP.view2 too, and your instructor can grade practical exams with it.

**New here?** Read [Getting started](getting-started.md), then [Viewing a slide](viewing.md). Taking a practical exam? Read [Exam answers](annotating.md#exam-answers) first.

**Using the keyboard only, a screen reader, larger text or high contrast?** See [Accessibility](accessibility.md).

> Most controls show what they do when you hover over them. Press **⇧⌘/** (Ctrl+Shift+/ on Windows) at any time to open this help.

## Your files stay yours

Slide Viewer never uploads your slides or annotations. The only thing it sends over the internet is its check for a new version on GitHub (see [Updates](getting-started.md#updates)).

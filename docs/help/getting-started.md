# Getting started

## What you need

- **A folder of slides.** Your course gives you whole-slide images, such as `VMS104.ndpi`. Put them all in one folder on your computer, or in subfolders of it (for example one subfolder per block or per lab). Slides on a USB drive or a network drive work too, but they load faster from your own disk.
- Nothing else. The slide readers are built into the app.

Slide files are large (often 1–3 GB each). If your course shares them as a `.zip`, unzip it first.

## Choose a folder

The first time you open Slide Viewer, click **Choose folder…** in the slide browser on the left, or choose **File ▸ Open Folder…** (**⌘O**, Ctrl+O on Windows). Pick the *parent* folder: the one that holds your slides or the subfolders they're in.

The app looks through that folder and every folder inside it for slides it can open (see [File formats](file-formats.md)). Large folders take a few seconds; a progress line shows how far it has got. Next time you open the app, it reopens the same folder.

To pick a different folder, choose **File ▸ Open Folder…** again.

## The slide browser

The browser on the left shows what was found, laid out like the folder on disk:

- **Folders** can be collapsed and expanded: click the folder's name. The app remembers which ones you closed.
- **Each slide** has a thumbnail, its file name and its size. Thumbnails are made the first time and remembered, so the second visit is instant.
- **A badge** on a slide means it already has annotations: you placed pins on it, or someone gave you an `.ndpa` file for it.
- **Search**: type in the box at the top to show only slides whose name contains what you typed, for example `104` or `liver`.

Click a slide to open it. Press **]** or **→** for the next slide in the list and **[** or **←** for the previous one, so you can flip through a block without touching the mouse.

Added or renamed slides while the app was open? Choose **File ▸ Rescan Folder** (**⌘R**).

Hide the browser to give the slide more room: click **Slides** at the top left, or press **⌥⌘1** (Ctrl+Alt+1).

## The window

- **Top bar**: the slide's name, **?** for this help, the preferences button, and **Panel**, which shows or hides the panel on the right.
- **Centre**: the slide itself, with the overview map, scale bar and zoom buttons over it (see [Viewing a slide](viewing.md)). The toolbar with the pan, pin, ruler and shape tools floats at the bottom.
- **Right panel**: two tabs. **Annotations** lists the pins and shapes on this slide (see [Pins and shapes](annotating.md)). **Adjust** has the image adjustments and stain tools (see [Adjustments and stains](adjustments-and-stains.md)). Show or hide it with **⌥⌘2** (Ctrl+Alt+2).

## Preferences

Choose **Slide Viewer ▸ Preferences…** (**⌘,**; on Windows **File ▸ Preferences…**, Ctrl+,). Preferences open in their own window and take effect straight away. They include how the mouse wheel zooms, whether the overview map and scale bar show, the colour of new pins, whether pin titles show on the slide, and which Auto adjustments run when a slide opens.

## Updates

When you open the app it quietly checks for a new version. If there is one, a message near the top offers **Install & restart** (your annotations are saved first) or **Later**. To check yourself, choose **Slide Viewer ▸ Check for Updates…** (on Windows, **File ▸ Check for Updates…**), or open **Preferences ▸ About**.
